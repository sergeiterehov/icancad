# ADR 0019 — Projected (derived) sketch geometry

Status: accepted (M2)

## Context

A sketch can be drawn only on its own plane, but the shape being designed often already exists in 3D: the outline of a face, a rim of a boss, a corner of a body. Redrawing that outline by hand is guesswork. Other CAD tools solve this with a "project/convert entities" tool that copies 3D geometry into the sketch and keeps the copy linked to its source.

The sketch model must stay serializable and independent from the kernel, a projected curve must survive the loss of its source, and the user must be able to turn a projection into ordinary geometry to edit it.

## Decisions

| Area              | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tool              | A `project` sketch tool sits after Bézier in the sketch rail (`Presentation` icon). While it is active the sketch's own geometry is inert (it cannot be clicked or dragged) and points, edges and faces of visible bodies become the picking targets. Clicking a face projects its boundary edges and their corner points; clicking an edge or a vertex projects just that element. The tool stays active for a sequence of picks.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Picking           | The viewport already picks body faces and edges. Projecting adds vertex picking: vertices are the endpoints of the B-rep edges, deduplicated by rounded position, and a vertex wins over an edge within an 8 px tolerance. The sketch's own section plane does not clip the model while projecting, because a clipped face cannot be clicked.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Geometry          | Points project along the sketch plane normal into points. Edges project into the simplest matching sketch curve: a line when collinear, a circle or ellipse when the projected loop fits one, an arc with the sweep direction of the source polyline, and a cubic Bézier otherwise. The conversion lives in `packages/sketch/src/projection.ts` and is kernel-agnostic. Projected entities are ordinary sketch geometry, not construction: they form profiles and extrudable regions like any curve, and the construction button toggles them like any other entity. The construction flag is the user's choice and a link refresh keeps it. |
| Vertex ids        | A projected corner is linked to the edges that meet there (`v:<edge>+<edge>`), not to its coordinates: resizing the body keeps those edges, so the corner follows the model. Links written before this rule carry the old position id; they keep working while the vertex stays in place, and projecting the face again replaces the ones a resize has broken.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Link and snapshot | `SketchFeature.projections` maps a sketch entity to `{ kind: 'vertex'                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | 'edge', bodyId, elementId }`. The sketch entity itself is the snapshot of the projected geometry. A projection owns one entity, so a face contributes one link per edge and per vertex. |
| Rebuild           | When a sketch is evaluated, every link is resolved against the bodies as they exist at that point of the history and the entity is regenerated in place. Downstream operations, the viewport and the sketch editor all use the regenerated geometry, so a projection follows the model without a document edit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Lost link         | If the body, the vertex or the edge is gone, the stored snapshot stays in place and the entity id is reported as lost. While the sketch is edited, geometry with a lost link is drawn in orange, the sketch row in the object tree gets an amber warning triangle and the operation chip in the timeline turns amber. The finished sketch object still shows the snapshot as ordinary geometry. This is a warning, not an error: the sketch stays usable and the link can still be detached.                                                                                                                                                 |
| Colour            | Linked projected geometry is violet and marked with violet list icons while the sketch is edited. In the finished sketch object the geometry is drawn like any other curve of that sketch, so the distinction is visible only in the editor.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Detach            | The sketch geometry context menu offers "Detach from source" (`Split` icon) for projected entities. Detaching removes the link only; the entity becomes ordinary local geometry and can be edited and constrained like any other. Deleting projected geometry removes its links.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Editing           | Projected entities behave like other sketch geometry: they are selected, listed, constrained and deleted the same way. They are not dragged, and the solver holds their control points fixed, so a constraint between projected and local geometry moves the local side; without this the solver would move a projection and the next rebuild would silently undo the constraint.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

## Consequences

- The document keeps the last projected geometry as a snapshot, so a project file opens with the right shapes even before the kernel rebuilds.
- A sketch made only of projections extrudes like any other sketch: the topology and the profiles see projected entities as ordinary curves.
- `Project` schema version 2 gains an optional `projections` array; old files load with an empty list.
- The warning state is derived during the rebuild and published with the scene; it is not stored in the document.
- Persistent naming for faces and edges would make links survive more edits; until then the id-based link plus snapshot is the contract.
